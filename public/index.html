    How to use Paperscape

    Plan a submission

    1. Find a venue. Open Conferences and filter by research area or month. Switch between the Map, Timeline and List views and click a conference to see every deadline.
    2. Make a plan. Click Plan for this venue, pick a backup, and use Suggest milestones to schedule backwards from the deadline.
    3. Stay on track. On Plans, tick milestones off as you go and watch the countdowns. Warnings appear if two deadlines clash or there is too little time to travel.

    Run a literature review

    1. Ask a question. On Reviews, create a review around your research question.
    2. Collect papers. Search recent papers by keyword, area and year, read the abstracts and add the relevant ones.
    3. Make sense of them. Record each paper's status, notes and key findings, link papers into threads, and see which ones cite each other.
    • No sign-up. Your plans and reviews are saved privately for this browser. Clearing site data or switching browsers starts a fresh workspace.
    • Deadlines are Anywhere on Earth (AoE), the convention most conferences use. "TBD" means the conference hasn't announced it yet.
    • Always up to date. Conference dates refresh automatically. Spot a dead link? Use Report a broken link in the conference panel.
